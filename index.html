Approach

A defined process with clear accountability at every stage.

  1. PHASE 01

    Discovery

    We document your objectives, constraints, and current systems, and advise candidly on whether custom software is the right investment.

  2. PHASE 02

    Scope and proposal

    You receive a written statement of work covering deliverables, schedule, and a fixed price before any engineering begins.

  3. PHASE 03

    Build and review

    Working software is demonstrated weekly, so stakeholders review real progress and direct priorities throughout delivery.

  4. PHASE 04

    Launch and support

    We manage release, transfer full ownership of code and accounts to you, and remain available for maintenance and future phases.

Frequently asked

What clients ask before engaging us.

What does RNV-TECH LLC do?
RNV-TECH LLC is a custom software development company. We design, build, and support mobile applications, internal tools and platforms, AI integrations, and backend systems for client organizations, and we publish our own product, MySecretary: Voice to action.
Who owns the software once it is delivered?
You do. Source code, data, and all service accounts are transferred to your organization at delivery.
How are projects priced?
Project work is quoted as a fixed price against a written statement of work. Retainers are billed monthly.
Will you sign a non-disclosure agreement?
Yes. A mutual NDA can be executed before the discovery conversation.
What support is available after launch?
Maintenance and further development are available under a managed retainer, or you may take the software in-house.
What is MySecretary: Voice to action?
MySecretary: Voice to action is our own voice-first AI productivity app for iPhone. One voice note becomes email drafts, task lists, meeting notes, summaries, action items, outlines, and daily briefings. It is available on the App Store.
