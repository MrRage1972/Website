A note from the founder

I did not start RNV-TECH from a quiet office or a startup incubator.

I started it while managing some of the busiest nightlife and restaurant environments in Los Angeles: ten to twelve hour shifts, moving nonstop, solving problems in real time, managing staff, handling guests, and constantly thinking three steps ahead.

Like most operators, I lived inside organized chaos. And every day, ideas were getting lost. Important contacts. Business opportunities. Follow-ups. The problem was never a lack of effort. The problem was friction.

Most software is built for people sitting at desks with time to slow down. Operators do not work that way. So I built the system I wished existed, and it became MySecretary: Voice to action.

Software should be judged on the busiest night of the year, not in the demonstration.

Building and running our own product taught us what it takes to carry software from an idea to something people rely on every day: the design, the engineering, the billing, the security, and the support after launch.

I also bring more than 15 years in security, both physical and cyber. It is why protecting your data and your customers is part of the design from the first day, not something added at the end.

RNV-TECH now brings that same discipline to client work. When you engage us, you work directly with me. I will tell you plainly what software can solve, what it cannot, and what it will cost before any work begins.

Michael Bettis Founder and Chief Executive Officer
